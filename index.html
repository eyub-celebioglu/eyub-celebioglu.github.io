Research

Autonomous Navigation in Uncertain Environments: RRT Planning and DQN Path Following

Eyub Celebioglu, Rahma Ouanennas, Yoann Morau · supervised by Olivier Buffet
Master's research project (first year), Université de Lorraine, 2026
report (PDF, in French)

A sampling-based planner (RRT) gives the robot a route; a reinforcement learning controller learns to follow it while avoiding obstacles. My part was the 2D simulator (kinematics, collisions, 5-ray LIDAR) and the DQN controller in PyTorch. Over three training versions I diagnosed a reward-hacking behaviour (the agent spinning in place), recalibrated the penalties, and oversampled rare collisions in the replay buffer. The team also compared RRT, RRT* and RRTSC, a shortcut variant we proposed.

DQN: 73–91% success per map, 3000 episodes, 5 maps, random starts
RRTSC: 14 vs 55 path nodes (−70%) vs RRT, a fraction of RRT* runtime
